# Tomato Splatter — 히어로 이미지 신규 생성 프롬프트

이 프롬프트는 기존 생성 이미지를 입력하거나 참조하지 않는 **완전 신규 생성용**이다.

## 표현 목적

고품질 베이컨 헤어 Roblox 아바타가 서로 다른 역할과 형태를 가진 토마토 무리의 중심에서 휠윈드를 사용하고, 토마토 주스와 전리품이 쏟아지는 Tomato Splatter의 핵심 플레이 순간을 표현한다.

## 생성 프롬프트

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: 16:9 hero gameplay image for a premium Roblox action-RPG pitch deck

Create a completely new image from scratch. Do not use or imitate any previous generated image.

SCENE
A high-quality Roblox R15 Bacon Hair avatar performs a powerful 360-degree whirlwind attack at the center of a dense tomato enemy crowd. The scene takes place in a compact private battle arena with a simple dark stone ground. The environment is deliberately restrained so that the combat, enemy types, attack radius and dropped items remain immediately readable.

PLAYER CHARACTER
Use an unmistakable Roblox R15 avatar with authentic Roblox proportions and segmented joints:
- square tan Roblox head
- classic simple Roblox smile
- recognizable layered brown Bacon Hair
- blue jacket over a dark shirt
- dark pants
- rectangular Roblox hands, arms, legs and boots
- premium materials, clean edges and high-quality lighting

The avatar must remain clearly Roblox. Do not turn the avatar into a realistic human, anime character, miniature figure or voxel character. The avatar holds a large fantasy sword and rotates through a full circular whirlwind attack.

TOMATO SURFACE AND MATERIAL
Every tomato must be modeled with smooth continuous curved surfaces and rounded organic volume.

Tomato skin must have:
- smooth subdivision-quality curvature
- soft natural asymmetry
- subtle dimples and ribbing appropriate to each tomato variety
- thin natural skin with a restrained waxy sheen
- small realistic surface variation rather than geometric facets
- naturally curved green leaves and stems

Tomatoes must look like stylized living tomatoes, not cubes, polygons, voxels, faceted low-poly spheres, bricks, toys or carved stone. Their surface must remain visibly smooth even in the foreground.

FOUR ENEMY TYPES
Show four visually distinct tomato roles based on recognizable tomato varieties. Each type must have a different silhouette, scale and combat behavior.

1. BASIC TYPE — CHERRY TOMATOES
- numerous small, smooth, round cherry tomatoes
- move in dense packs
- bright red skin
- very simple small eyes and a restrained determined expression
- these form most of the crowd

2. RANGED TYPE — ROMA TOMATOES
- fewer tall, elongated Roma tomatoes
- clearly vertical oval silhouette
- long flexible vine stems raised in an attack wind-up
- a few tomato seeds or seed-shaped projectiles forming near the raised vine
- readable as a ranged threat from a distance

3. PRISM TYPE — GOLDEN HEIRLOOM TOMATOES
- rare medium-sized golden-orange heirloom tomatoes
- naturally irregular rounded shape
- smooth translucent skin with a subtle internal prismatic light effect
- small Coin glints around them
- visually valuable without becoming crystalline rocks
- their bodies must still look soft, curved and unmistakably tomato-like

4. ELITE TYPE — BEEFSTEAK TOMATOES
- a few very large deep-crimson beefsteak tomatoes
- broad flattened body with strong natural ribs and folds
- thick leafy green calyx that creates a powerful silhouette
- restrained gem-colored growths embedded near the outer skin
- visually tough and important without wearing armor, clothing or a crown

COMBAT ACTION
The avatar stands inside the enemy ring and performs one complete 360-degree sword whirlwind.

- show the full circular attack radius
- use one clean ivory-gold weapon trail
- tomatoes touching the blade squash, split and release tomato pulp, seeds and ketchup-like juice
- defeated tomatoes visibly break apart as tomato flesh and skin
- intact enemies remain outside the active attack ring
- preserve clear separation between the avatar, active hit area, surviving enemies and dropped items

The red liquid must unmistakably read as tomato juice and ketchup because seeds, tomato pulp and pieces of tomato skin are visible. It must never resemble blood or gore.

LOOT
Behind the cleared attack arc, show:
- scattered gold Coin
- two or three dropped weapons
- one rare sword marked by a narrow vertical violet light beam

The rare drop must be noticeable without overpowering the combat.

COMPOSITION
- 16:9 landscape
- elevated three-quarter gameplay camera
- full whirlwind circle visible
- Bacon Hair avatar at the visual center
- dense enemy crowd surrounding the full attack radius
- Basic enemies are numerous
- Ranged and Prism enemies are sparse priority targets
- Elite enemies are large visual anchors
- leave a small area of clean negative space for slide copy
- the image must remain understandable when reduced to a presentation-slide thumbnail

VISUAL QUALITY
Polished, expressive, high-fidelity real-time Roblox game target for an adult action-RPG audience. Use sophisticated lighting, strong value separation, controlled effects and carefully authored silhouettes. The Roblox avatar geometry and the organic tomato geometry must have clearly different surface languages: angular segmented avatar, smooth curved tomatoes.

COLOR
- dark neutral stone arena
- natural tomato reds and leaf greens
- restrained golden-orange Prism enemies
- ivory-gold whirlwind trail
- small violet rare-loot accent
- avoid rainbow saturation

CONSTRAINTS
- no input or visual reference to previous generated images
- no farm, barn, windmill, village, castle or unrelated scenery
- no identical tomato clones
- no cube-shaped tomatoes
- no voxel tomatoes
- no faceted polygon tomato surfaces
- no low-poly tomato spheres
- no brick or toy texture on tomatoes
- no glowing eyes, fangs, demonic faces or horror
- no exaggerated screaming emoji expressions
- no realistic human protagonist
- no anime protagonist
- no blood or gore
- no UI
- no text
- no logo
- no watermark
- do not imitate characters, enemies, weapons or environments from another game
```

## 입력 방법

- 기존 PNG를 첨부하지 않는다.
- 이미지 편집이나 스타일 변환이 아니라 새 이미지 생성으로 실행한다.
- `referenced_image_paths`와 최근 대화 이미지 참조 기능을 사용하지 않는다.
- 첫 결과에서 토마토 표면이 각져 보이면 기존 결과를 수정하지 말고, 같은 프롬프트에서 `smooth subdivision-quality curvature`와 `continuous organic surface`를 다시 강조하여 신규 생성한다.
